Special Posting

Advantages of SPECIAL POSTING

  • i. Special Posting gives you 100% assurance of knowing the ORGANIZATION you are to be posted even before the Appointment Letters are released online.
  • ii. With the SPECIAL POSTING, you can choose an ORGANIZATION within your catchment area so you won't go and pay a huge amount for accommodation elsewhere.
  • iii. If you decide to choose an Institution/Organization which is not in your town or city, you still get the advantage to prepare well before the National Service starts. You will know the organization already — so it makes you prepare for accommodation before the Appointment Letters are released.
  • iv. Sometimes, your monthly allowance delays. So if you get the Organization/Institution of your Choice, you will not encounter problems even if the Allowance delays.

How It Works

  1. Fill in the form with your details
  2. Complete the MoMo payment
  3. We submit your posting request
  4. Receive confirmation within 24 hours
